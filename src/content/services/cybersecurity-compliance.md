### Compliance and Security Standards

#### GDPR

Controls and practices designed to support the protection and privacy of personal data.

#### CCPA

Privacy and consumer-protection requirements applicable to qualifying organizations handling California residents’ personal information.

#### HIPAA

Security and privacy requirements relevant to protected health information and healthcare operations.

#### PCI-DSS

Security requirements for organizations that store, process, or transmit payment card information.
