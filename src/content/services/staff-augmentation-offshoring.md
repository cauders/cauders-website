# 16. Staff Augmentation and Offshoring

Cauders provides access to experienced, pre-vetted technology professionals who can work alongside your internal teams or operate as dedicated delivery teams.

### Staffing models

- Individual specialist placement
- Dedicated development teams
- Project-based teams
- Offshore delivery teams
- Nearshore and distributed teams
- Short-term capacity expansion
- Long-term technical staffing
- Managed technical teams

### Areas of expertise

#### Web and Mobile Development

Developers who build responsive websites, web applications, native mobile apps, and cross-platform solutions.

#### Data-Driven Solutions

Data analysts, engineers, architects, and scientists who turn data into reliable insights and business outcomes.

#### Digital Transformation

Specialists who modernize infrastructure, improve customer experiences, digitize processes, and increase organizational agility.

#### DevOps

Engineers who improve development workflows, CI/CD pipelines, deployment processes, infrastructure automation, and operational reliability.

#### Data and AI

Professionals experienced in machine learning, analytics, predictive modeling, generative AI, data engineering, and AI implementation.

#### Business Applications

Specialists who develop, integrate, customize, and maintain ERP, CRM, service management, and other enterprise systems.

- Flexible team scaling
- Access to specialized expertise

- Lower training requirements
- Extended delivery capacity
- Global talent access
- Support across time zones
