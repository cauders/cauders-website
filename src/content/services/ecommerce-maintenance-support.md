# 6. E-Commerce Maintenance and Support

### Store management services

- Product uploads and updates
- Product descriptions and categorization
- Inventory updates
- Order and discount management
- Store configuration
- Customer account support
- Technical troubleshooting
- Performance optimization
- Security updates
- Backup and recovery support

### Ongoing Enhancements

We continuously improve store functionality, integrations, design, performance, and customer experience as your business and market requirements evolve.

We organize product data, categories, filters, menus, and content hierarchies to help customers find products quickly and complete purchases efficiently.
