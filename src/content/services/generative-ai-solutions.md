### Vector Semantic Search

We build search experiences that understand meaning and user intent rather than depending only on exact keyword matches. This enables faster and more accurate retrieval across documents, products, support content, and enterprise knowledge.

### Autonomous AI Agents

We design AI agents capable of performing defined tasks, coordinating workflows, using approved tools, and adapting to changing information under appropriate human oversight and governance.
