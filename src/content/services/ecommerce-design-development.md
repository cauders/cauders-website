We design frictionless shopping journeys across product discovery, search, filtering, product details, cart, checkout, payment, account management, and post-purchase support.

### Headless Storefront Development

We separate the customer-facing storefront from the commerce backend, enabling greater design flexibility, performance, and integration options through APIs and modern frontend frameworks.

### E-Commerce Revamps and Migrations

We redesign or migrate stores while protecting data integrity, SEO performance, product information, customer records, and business continuity.

### E-Commerce Compliance

We help implement security and privacy measures aligned with applicable requirements, including:

- GDPR
- CCPA
- PCI-DSS
- SSL/TLS encryption
- Secure payment processing
- Access control
- Data protection policies
- Consent and privacy management

Compliance requirements depend on your industry, location, business model, and data-processing activities. Our technical implementation can support your compliance program but does not replace legal advice or formal certification.
